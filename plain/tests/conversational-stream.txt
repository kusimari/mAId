USER: i was reading about merkle trees yesterday and one thing i still can't quite intuit — what's the practical advantage of merkle proofs over just hashing the whole dataset and comparing?
AGENT: Logarithmic size. With n items, a full-dataset hash is one O(n) compute and O(1) storage to compare; a merkle proof is O(log n) storage to *verify* a single item against a known root. That's the whole point — you don't need to ship the dataset.
