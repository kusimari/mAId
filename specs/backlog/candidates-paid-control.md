# Backlog: candidates-paid-control

## What

Show with a real model whether candidates in the prefix change what the
model does, not only that the habit reaches the prefix.

## Why

Stream 11 (`prompt-prefix`) spent 6 paid runs on two habits, one with
and one against the model's usual fix. All 6 kept the habit, with
candidates on and off. The replies cannot say why: the model may have
searched the store itself in the off arm (the genome names it and the
memory tools are allowed), or it follows a saved habit however it finds
it. The free tests prove the mechanism only.
