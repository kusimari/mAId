# Backlog: harness-source-declared

## What

Let the harness's own source be declared rather than found by name. The
core files a proposal about `harness` (the core, the reflector, the
reflect skill) on the source of the bring-in named `claude-adapter`.
Another shell's adapter, or a part that ships the core under another
name, gets "its source (not recorded)". A contract field (say, on the
adapter's declaration: this unit ships the core) would remove the literal.

## Why

Left from the sleep-and-adaptation stream (ringmaster review: a
declaration field later). The literal ties the core to mAId's installer
naming.
